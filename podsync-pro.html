PODSync Pro

Updated 18 August 2026

What the free plugin already does

Products, prices and availability copied from Printful. Simple products and the first variant. Hourly and on-demand sync, with a plain-language log.

Pro is a separate plugin that sits on top of the free one. It adds work the free plugin does not do; it never switches anything off. If the free sync is all you need, use the free one.

What Pro adds

  • Every size and colour becomes a real WooCommerce variation, each with its own price, code and stock
  • Paid orders sent to Printful — held for your approval by default, and they arrive there as drafts, so nothing goes into production until you confirm it
  • Live shipping rates at checkout, off until you switch them on. If Printful is slow or unsure, no rate is added and your own shipping methods are used — the cart is never held up

Price

  • One shop$39 / year
  • Five shops$89 / year

A licence covers updates and support for a year. The plugin keeps working when it lapses — only updates and support stop. One shop per licence, five for the larger one; you can move a shop from your Polar customer portal.

Before you buy

Install the free plugin first and let one sync run. If it copies your products the way you expect, Pro will too — it uses the same code underneath. If the free sync has trouble on your shop, tell us before paying: support@podsync.lifeinsight-365.com.
