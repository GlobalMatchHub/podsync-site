The parts people ask about

Will this put my products on ChatGPT?

No plugin can. Those channels admit merchants themselves, and not every shop is eligible yet. What this does is make sure that when you apply, and when the bar moves, you are not turned down for your data.

Does the free plugin lose anything when Pro exists?

No, and it never will. All thirty-five checks, all three channels, the whole feed. Pro adds. If you stop paying, your shop goes back to exactly what it does now, with the history you built kept until you delete it.

Does it change my products?

No. It reads. Every fix is yours to make, on your own product screens.

What if you disappear?

Your shop keeps working. The channel requirements are bundled inside the plugin and the last set it trusted keeps being used. Nothing depends on our server being up.
